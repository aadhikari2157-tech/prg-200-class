Priority scoring algorithm
Priority Score = (days until deadline × 2) + estimated hours − importance weight
High importance = −10  ·  Medium = −5  ·  Low = −1 Lower score = higher urgency
Assignment (1 day, High, 2h)
(1×2) + 2 − 10 = –6 ← top priority
Club Meeting (3 days, Med, 1h)
(3×2) + 1 − 5 = 2
Project Report (7 days, High, 5h)
(7×2) + 5 − 10 = 9 ← lowest priority
